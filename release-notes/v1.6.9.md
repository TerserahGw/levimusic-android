# LeviMusic - Aurelis Online v1.6.9 stable

- Standard now captures the active world `BlockSource` from Minecraft's own verified block-query path instead of relying on guessed Actor and Dimension offsets.
- Room and cave probes use that live receiver to classify enclosed spaces and synchronize the result with Bridge.
- Immersive Sound and 3D Lyrics Beats remain exclusive to Standard.

Standard mode requires the matching v1.6.9 Bridge APK.
