# LeviMusic - Aurelis Online v1.7.1 stable

- Restores authentic word-by-word lyric timing from QRC, nested TTML, enhanced LRC, Bridge, Aurelis, and imported offline lyrics.
- Improves Japanese, Chinese, and Korean lyric presentation: short CJK lines are split into timed pieces instead of appearing as one full line.
- Adds Minecraft Style and Smooth 3D lyric fonts. Minecraft Style is the default, uses a slightly coarser pixel look, and no longer adds a black outline.
- Reworks Now Playing with a centered five-line lyric timeline, word progress, smaller romanized readings, and separate Search, Queue, and Playlist tabs.
- Preserves per-word markers when saving lyrics offline and fixes missing spaces between Latin enhanced-LRC tokens.
- Improves lyric matching for decorated video titles such as `[4K]`, `Official Video`, and `Lyrics Video`.
- Adds curated per-song 3D lyric color themes and smoother in-place lyric transitions without exit snapping or blinking.
- Lets the compact HUD pass drag gestures to LeviLauncher's Customize Controls editor.

LeviMusic remains one unified package: it uses the Bridge automatically when paired and Aurelis fallback when unpaired. Cinematic AFK View and Immersive Sound remain experimental/WIP.
