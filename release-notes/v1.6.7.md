# LeviMusic - Aurelis Online v1.6.7 stable

- Standard now applies cave reflections and underwater filtering directly to decoded PCM, so the immersive effect is preserved in internal screen recordings.
- Standalone no longer crashes while constructing Android immersive-audio effects and adds a recordable early cave reflection alongside the system reverb tail.
- Room and cave detection now holds its result briefly across chunk edges instead of repeatedly creating and destroying the reverb chain.
- Cave reverb has stronger early reflections, a longer decay, and a clearer indoor character.
- Music pauses automatically when leaving a world and resumes only when returning if LeviMusic performed the pause.
- Exiting Minecraft stops Bridge playback and its foreground service; a heartbeat timeout handles abrupt game exits.

Standard mode requires the matching v1.6.7 Bridge APK.
